webpify

Converts the images you upload to WebP and the videos to MP4, on the sites you choose, before the site receives them.

    Conversion

    Applies to every site. Changes save as you make them.

    Higher keeps more detail and makes bigger files. 80 suits most photos.

    Bigger images are scaled down to fit. 0 keeps the original size.

    Videos

    Videos become H.264 MP4 files, made with your browser's own encoder. The sound is kept as it is whenever an MP4 can hold it.

    Bigger videos are scaled down to fit. Frame rates above 30 are brought down to 30.

    Standard suits videos shown on web pages. If the MP4 wouldn't be smaller than the original, the original is kept.
