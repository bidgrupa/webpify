# Privacy policy for webpify

Last updated: 2026-10-13

webpify converts images to WebP on your own device, right before you upload them on websites you have chosen. It does not collect, store or send any personal data.

## Images you upload

When you pick or drop images in an upload field on a site you added in webpify's settings, the extension converts them inside your browser. The images are never sent anywhere by webpify. The converted files go only where you were already uploading them: to the website you are using, as part of your own upload.

The converted files keep the original's colour profile. Other embedded information, such as camera details and the location where a photo was taken, is removed.

## Settings

Your settings are stored in your browser's local extension storage on this device and are not synced:
- conversion quality
- maximum image size
- the lossless, "keep the original" and "keep graphics sharp" options
- the list of sites and upload fields
- when a pause you started from the toolbar ends

Removing a site deletes it from that list. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its settings.

## Site access

webpify can only see pages on sites you turn on, from its toolbar icon or its settings. Your browser asks for your permission for each site. When you click the toolbar icon, webpify reads the address of the current tab so it can offer to turn itself on there. The address is only saved, in the site list above, if you turn webpify on for that site, and it is never sent anywhere. You can revoke site access at any time from the toolbar icon, the extension's settings, or your browser's extension settings.

## What webpify does not do

- No analytics, tracking or telemetry.
- No network requests of its own and no third-party services.
- No data is sold or shared with anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in this repository, or write to the contact address on the extension's store page.
